## Services
1. **Gutter Installation & Spouting Repairs:** Spouting installation and repair.
2. **Professional Gutter Cleaning:** Vacuum gutter cleaning services.
3. **Roof Maintenance & Roofing Repairs:** Maintenance & repairs for roofing systems.

## Products (Gutter Profiles)
- **Box Profile:** Designed for heavy rainfall intensity, allowing fewer downpipes.
- **Quad Profile:** Designed and tested specifically for the South Island environment.
- **Half Round Profile:** Perfect for classic kiwi homes to unique architectural designs.

## Observations & Issues with Current Site
The current site appears to be built on Wix and has several inconsistencies and broken elements that we can improve upon in our new, premium design.

> [!WARNING]
> **Discrepancies Found:**
> - **Experience:** The Home Page states "20+ years experience", whereas the Services Page mentions "over 15 years".
> - **Warranty/Guarantee:** The Services Page offers a "10-year leak-free guarantee", while the Contact Page states they back their spouting with a "full 5 year warantee" (spelled with a typo).
> - **Broken Links:** The phone number link on the Contact Page points to the home page instead of `tel:`. Social media links point to default Wix placeholder accounts.
> - **Form Inconsistencies:** The contact form on the Home Page has a single "Name" field, while the Contact Page form splits this into "First Name" and "Last Name".
